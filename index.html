Password Reset

Having trouble with your password? Here's how to reset it:

For Students

  1. Go to the student portal login page
  2. Click "Forgot Password"
  3. Enter your school email address
  4. Check your email for reset instructions
  5. Create a new strong password

For Parents & Teachers

  1. Visit the staff portal login
  2. Select "Reset Password"
  3. Enter your registered email
  4. Follow the instructions sent to your email
  5. Contact IT if problems continue
